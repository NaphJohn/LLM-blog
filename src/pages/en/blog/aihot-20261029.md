## ★ Most Worth Your Attention Today

**vLLM took another step at "cutting cost," while SGLang stood still at "exposing risk."**

In the same day, same frame, the contrast between the two lines is what you should act on today:

1. **vLLM**: v0.33 is wrapping up its RC, shipping **v0.33.0rc1** today; the mainline **NVFP4 4-bit KV** drops same-card long-context capacity to about **1/4** (measured: MiniMax M3.1 Flash 4×B300 @256K concurrent sequences 28→76, about **2.7×**; per-token KV 61,440→34,560 bytes, **-43.75%**).
2. **SGLang**: still **v0.5.22 (10-19)**, no v0.5.23 in-window; more importantly it still carries **CVE-2026-111391** — `dumper _evaluate_filter()` calls `eval` directly, causing **RCE (CVSS 9.2 v4 / 8.1 v3)**, affecting **0.5.10–0.5.22**, with multiple verifications confirming **no fixed version to date**.

**Actionable conclusion**: if you run SGLang with Dumper on, **immediately `unset DUMPER_SERVER_PORT` and block public `/dumper/configure`** (track issue #44188); for NVFP4 long-context, prefer vLLM v0.33 mainline — this round SGLang is clearly behind at "getting the job done."

> Worth saying separately: SGLang has **gone ~10 days without a release** yet sits on a CVSS-9.2 unpatched RCE, while vLLM keeps landing the low-precision KV route inside its RC. The two frameworks' low-precision KV paths are converging (both heading to NVFP4 ~4× density), but the architectural fork — "SGLang exposes KV explicitly to an external router, vLLM keeps it built-in via MRV2" — is widening the gap on reliability and attack surface.

### Under the Hood: How NVFP4 4-bit KV Quadruples Density

**The path**: `vo_split` breaks a 512-wide head into two 256s, per-layer **amax/6.0 calibration** + `llm-compressor` + `compressed-tensors` on disk, density ~**4×** (with scale blocks ~**4.5 bit/token**).

The cost is clear: **a calibration error makes the model diverge** (on a 5090 the error can reach the 0.0047 magnitude). That means NVFP4 is not "turn it on and save" — it is "save only if calibrated right" — so re-test KV accuracy on real load before going live.

## 3. AI Papers & Industry Hotspots

### Paper Core: FlowVLA (HKUST-GZ, arXiv:2508.18269)

Inserts an explicit **optical-flow middle step `v_t → f_t → v_t+1`** into next-frame prediction. Three points: ① decouples motion reasoning from appearance generation (cures the pixel-copy trap); ② motion step directly supervised + frame-flow interleaved causal chain; ③ image/flow share a VQ-GAN tokenizer, action uses FAST.

Results: **LIBERO 88.1 / SimplerEnv 74.0 / real-robot 44.0**, **1/3 the steps to peak, 50% data +55%**. Impact: establishes the "explicit / supervised / on-the-causal-chain intermediate representation" triad, making the world model a **physical-prior engine** for the action head.

### Operator: DFlash Block Diffusion Draft (Z Lab, ICML 2026)

The draft side swaps autoregressive for a **5-layer block diffusion**, one forward pass emitting the whole block in parallel plus target-model hidden-state KV injected per layer; **lossless >6×**, vs at most 2.5× for EAGLE-3; integrated into vLLM/SGLang, draft heads available for Qwen3.5/3.6/Kimi K2.5.

### Performance: KV Eviction (H2O / SnapKV)

"Simply don't store some KV": **H2O** heavy-hitter + sliding window (keep 20% for 29× throughput); **SnapKV** locks the KV pool at prefill end via an observation window (16K gen 3.6× / memory 8.2×); on reasoning models **SnapKV-Decoding** is optimal. This is a complementary "save KV memory" route to NVFP4 4-bit KV.
