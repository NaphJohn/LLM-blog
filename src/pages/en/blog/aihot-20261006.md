### Under the Hood: vLLM v0.31.0 #57632 — DFlash Context K/V Frozen Once in the Draft Graph (dedup recompute at graph capture)

- **Approach**: DFlash's per-step-recomputed context K/V is **computed only once and frozen during draft CUDA graph capture**, and every subsequent step computes **only the new candidate token's attention**; paired with **MRV2 async scheduling (#58065)** so draft and verify **overlap** on the pipeline.
- **Benefit**: directly reusable for this author's **OpenInfer/Qwen3-4B DFlash** — lower per-step draft cost, stabilize acceptance, raise end-to-end decode throughput; the cost is correctness leaning on whether graph-internal state freeze is complete (recall last round's #56158 FULL-graph-replay-bypasses-Python-guard mine).

> My read: v0.31.0 bundles "DFlash async + freeze context K/V in graph + MRV2 async scheduling" into a stable default path — another solid landing of moving spec-decode bookkeeping from the eager path onto GPU-Native/graph; isomorphic to this author's OpenInfer/DFlash/DSpark — both center on "lower per-step draft cost, overlap draft/verify". During the migration the old path (FULL graph) can still bite at long-context high concurrency, so last round's #56158 fix stays high-priority.
