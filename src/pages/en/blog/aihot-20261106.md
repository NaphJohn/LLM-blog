## ★ Most Worth Your Attention Today

**vLLM fixed a cache pitfall you never knew was biting you: PD disaggregation × speculative decoding silently swallow prefix-cache hits, inflating warm TTFT by 31%.** This is the most deployable engineering fix in 72 hours (PR #64096, merged to main on 11-03).

Three layers of fact:

1. **Symptom**: PD disaggregation relies on prefix-KV reuse, but EAGLE / MTP drafters, when matching the prefix, **drop the final lookahead hash block** — the hit rate silently drops, and what you see is "PD + MTP is slower than expected," not an error.
2. **Fix**: PR #64096 extends lookahead block hashes to **three KV connectors — NIXL / Mooncake / offloading**; MultiConnector only enables when each sub-connector **opts in**.
3. **Gain**: under NIXL P / D, warm TTFT **1173 → 809ms**; under Mooncake, **1236 → 931ms** (concurrency 64, MTP k=4), with GSM8K / GPQA held flat → **deployments running PD + MTP should upgrade**.

**Actionable conclusion**: if you run PD disaggregation + speculative decoding, go check right now whether your connector hit rate is being silently eaten; PD + MTP deployments on NIXL / Mooncake can pick up −31% warm TTFT for free by moving to a main build carrying #64096, with no accuracy loss.

> Worth saying separately: this PR exposes a hidden conflict where the two mainstream optimization routes — "**disaggregation**" and "**speculation**" — **interfere with each other's caching**. When two optimizations that both rewrite the KV prefix are on simultaneously, they don't error or crash; they just discount each other's gains. The hit rate of a combined play must be watched with a dedicated metric, not inferred from end-to-end latency alone.

On the papers side, today's read is a piece of "archaeology": **DeepMind Gato (2022)** — a single 1.18B Transformer that compresses the perception / language / action of 604 tasks into one 1024-token sequence. What it proved was not "can do everything," but "**interfaces can be unified**" — the syntax of every VLA base today still derives from it (see section 3).

### Under the Hood: vLLM #64096 — When "Disaggregation" Meets "Speculation," Who Steals the Cache

The most worthwhile technical read of the day.

**The conflict**: PD disaggregation saves prefill via prefix-KV reuse; EAGLE / MTP drafters, when matching the prefix, drop the final **lookahead hash block** — so the hit rate drops "imperceptibly," without an error.

**The fix**: extend lookahead block hashes to three KV connectors (NIXL / Mooncake / offloading), with MultiConnector enabling only when each sub-connector **opts in** → recover the wasted cache hits, **TTFT −31% with no accuracy loss** (GSM8K / GPQA flat).

## 3. AI Papers & Industry Hotspots (Sources Present This Issue)

### Core Paper

**Gato / A Generalist Agent** (DeepMind, arXiv:2205.06175, TMLR 2022) — a **single decoder-only Transformer** doing next-token prediction across the perception / language / action of 604 tasks.

**Core ideas**:

1. **Everything is a token, but each modality has its own vocabulary slice**: text 32,000 / discrete [0,1024) / continuous μ-law 1024 bins → [32000,33024) / image 16×16 patches.
2. **The loss mask covers only actions and text** — it is a policy, not a world model.
3. **During training, 25% of sequences are prefixed with a same-task demo**, turning imitation into in-context imitation.

**Impact**: of 604 tasks, 450+ exceed the 50%-of-expert threshold (vendor metric); real-robot fine-tune **60% vs 0.5% from-scratch BC**. Two shortcomings (discrete precision, per-dimension autoregressive too slow) directly forked into **RT-2** (the semantic route) and **π0 / GR00T N2** (the continuous-action expert route).

### Operator Explainers

**μ-law companding + uniform-binning action tokenizer**: y = sgn(x)·ln(1+μ|x|)/ln(1+μ) (μ=255) then uniform binning; error bound Δ/2, RMS≈0.289Δ. **μ-law does not change the error magnitude; it changes the error's distribution on the value axis** — small-amplitude actions get higher resolution. In use: Gato 1024 bins, RT-1 11 dims × 256 bins at 3Hz, OpenVLA 7B + 256 bins over 970K episodes; the 2026 division of labor = Qwen3-VL-series backbone + discrete token or flow-matching expert.

### Performance Optimization

**Sequence Packing + variable-length attention**: replace padding with "multiple short samples packed into one long sequence" — the cheapest, side-effect-free throughput amplifier on the training side. NeMo (Llama 7B / Dolly) reaches up to **10×** FLOPs and up to **6×** shorter training without losing convergence; Tiptree online packing beats **>2×** on FLAN (merged into HF Transformers 4.44); at 90% padding it saves **89.73%** of attention compute; VLA-side industrial reports cite **1.88×**. **Key pitfall**: complexity must stay Σs_i², not (Σs_i)² — a dense mask that blocks cross-sample attention makes long packs slower, and position ids must reset per subsequence.
