### Under the Hood: SGLang #40826 Candidate-Token Scoring (less speculative-decode waste)

- **Approach**: after the draft net generates block / tree candidates, **each candidate is scored individually and calibrated into comparable confidence**, and low-confidence candidates are **pruned before verify** — cutting "rejected-after-verify" waste. Verification itself has a cost; blocking doomed candidates before verification is pure gain.
- **Same source**: isomorphic to this author's OpenInfer/DFlash/DSpark (Qwen3-4B) candidate selector — both believe "how candidates are ranked and calibrated" decides E[L]; suggest reproducing per-item calibration against accept-length gains.
- **Sideways**: vLLM's online acceptance estimator (#52228) is a "post-verify" estimate, while SGLang's PR **moves** scoring **pre-verify** — one corrects after the fact, the other prunes in advance.

> My read: the speculative-decode "candidate selector" is not decoration. #40826 moving scoring forward proves it — rather than passively accepting rejects at the verification stage, actively prune before verification. That is exactly what the DFlash candidate selector has been doing; another engineering isomorphism surfaces today.

### Today's Highlight (1 sentence)

### Paper Core (RoboBrain 2.0 · BAAI, arXiv:2507.02029)

- **One-line positioning**: an embodied vision-language foundation model, the "robot brain" — unifying **perception, reasoning, and planning** into one model: input high-resolution image / multi-view / video / scene graph → unified token sequence, output task text, precise coordinates (pointing/trajectory), and a CoT reasoning chain.
- **Core approach**: **7B / 32B** dual versions, **Qwen2.5-VL initialization** (689M vision encoder + MLP projector + LLM decoder), fully open-sourced; three-stage training (basic spatiotemporal learning → 224K embodied spatiotemporal augmentation → CoT-SFT 195K + GRPO RFT 45K, composite reward = correctness + format).
- **Results**: **9 spatial benchmarks refreshed** (BLINK 83.95 / CV-Bench 85.75 / Where2Place 73.59), EgoPlan2 58.14, Multi-Robot-Plan 81.83, **beating GPT-4o / Gemini / Claude closed baselines**.
- **Impact**: the embodied "brain-cerebellum-body" layering takes shape — an open-source brain lowers the in-house R&D bar for whole-body makers and accelerates the "body + third-party brain" decoupled division of labor.

### Operator Deep-Dive: MoBA (Mixture-of-Block Attention, Moonshot Kimi, arXiv:2502.13189)

- **Positioning**: ports the **MoE routing idea into attention** — chop the context into blocks, and the query uses a **parameter-free top-k gate** to look only at the k most relevant KV blocks (O(N²) → O(N·k·B)); **1M context 6.5×, 10M 16×+**, RULER on par with full attention.
- **In use / related**: already deployed to back **Kimi's long context**; "collided" with **DeepSeek NSA** in the same month (NSA's learnable three-branch vs MoBA's parameter-free single mechanism) — needs continued training, not plug-and-play.

### Performance Optimization: SpecDec++ (adaptive draft length, Princeton, arXiv:2405.19715, COLM 2025)

- **Positioning**: formalizes "keep drafting vs commit-and-verify" as an **MDP** and proves the optimal policy is a **threshold policy** — the draft model adds an **acceptance prediction head** and stops when the predicted reject probability exceeds the threshold; on **llama-2-chat 7B&70B**, Alpaca **2.04×** / GSM8K **2.26×** / HumanEval **2.23×** (over fixed K by **+7.2~11.1%**).
- **Same family**: orthogonal puzzle piece with **JetSpec (manages method) and DSpark (manages field)**, managing the "**timing**" — in the same big direction as #40826's candidate scoring, "speculative-decode refinement."
