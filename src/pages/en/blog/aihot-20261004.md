- **Impact**: the preview-execute-correct loop favors long-horizon home / commercial service scenarios, and cross-embodiment transfer cuts per-body data cost.

### Operator Deep-Dive: Partial RoPE (Partial Rotary Position Encoding)

- **Positioning**: applies RoPE only to the **first 25% of head dims**, leaving the rest position-free to improve long-sequence extrapolation; **head_dim=256 ⇒ d_rope=64**; paired with **Zero-Centered RMSNorm, GQA**.
- **Significance**: full RoPE over-rotates and extrapolates unstably at very long context; adding position to only a few leading dims keeps local position-sensitivity while stabilizing the long range — steadier for VLA long video history, a standard trick for long-context (Qwen3-Next/3.5 Gated Attention).

### Performance Optimization: HeteroSpec (heterogeneity-aware speculative decoding, arXiv:2505.13254, PKU / SCITIX)

- **Positioning**: allocates **draft/verify resources dynamically by token verify difficulty** — easy tokens draft less / verify more, hard tokens draft more / verify less; **4 models / 5 benchmarks avg 4.26× speedup, lossless, no retraining**.
- **Same family**: orthogonal to **JetSpec (method layer), DSpark (system layer), SpecDec++ (threshold MDP)**, together serving VLA on-device low-latency control — in the same big direction as this author's OpenInfer/DFlash candidate selector, "speculative-decode refinement".
