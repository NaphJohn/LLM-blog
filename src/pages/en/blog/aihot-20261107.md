## ★ Most Worth Your Attention Today

### Under the Hood: vLLM #66064 Fuses Gated DeltaNet Prefill Into One Bandwidth-Bound Kernel

The most worthwhile technical read of the day.

PR **#66064** fuses Gated DeltaNet prefill's state gather/scatter from **4–5 eager kernels per layer** down to **a single bandwidth-bound kernel spanning all GDN prefill backends**:

- B300 upper-layer prefill chain **1.16–1.50×**
- Qwen3.5-0.8B forward prefill8192 **down 2.7%**, chunked prefill **down 6.1%**, decode unchanged

The essence is **fewer global-memory round trips** — intermediate state stays in registers / fast storage. It confirms once more: **kernel fusion is where PyTorch inference speed hides**, and it helps long-context prefill the most.

> My read: fusing several eager kernels into one bandwidth-bound kernel is the steadiest cost-down path in the long-context + hybrid-attention era. No new hardware required — just fewer HBM round trips, and HBM bandwidth is exactly the first bottleneck for large-scale MoE / prefill.

## 3. AI Papers & Industry Hotspots

### Paper Core · RT-H: Action Hierarchies Using Language

**Belkhale et al., Google DeepMind × Stanford, arXiv:2403.01823, RSS 2024 [embodied intelligence]**

- One-liner: insert a **language-action intermediate layer** between task and action (e.g. "move arm forward"), forcing the policy to learn the low-level motion structure shared across tasks.
- Core idea: ① single VLM, dual query (π_h language-action → π_l action), the RT-2 recipe; ② **2500+ language-action phrases** auto-extracted from proprioception, zero manual labeling; ③ the language-action layer = **data bus + human-language intervention interface**.
- Impact: the direct source of hierarchical VLA (Helix/DriveVLM/π0.5 share the dual-system paradigm); language-intervention fine-tuning beats teleoperation intervention (IWR) by ~**50%**.

### Operator Note · z-loss

**ST-MoE, Zoph et al., arXiv:2202.08906**: penalizes only the magnitude term logsumexp², c_z=1e-3; its zero sits at logZ=0, removing only the logit's common-shift degree of freedom without favoring any class; ST-MoE without it hangs 4 of 6, with it all 3 are stable; standard in PaLM/GLaM, same coefficient in OLMoE; orthogonal to the MoE aux loss.

### Performance Optimization · Data Mixing Laws

**Ye et al., arXiv:2403.16952**: L(h)=E+ΣC_i·exp(γ_i·h_i); 70M–410M small models ×30B ×40 ratio groups extrapolate the optimal 1B/100B ratio; the optimized ratio ≈ training **48%** more steps than the default; the real-machine / video / language data-ratio problem in VLA applies directly.
