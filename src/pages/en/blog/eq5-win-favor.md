## 0. What This Chapter Solves

Chapters 1-4 solved "stable, attractive, understanding, de-escalating." This chapter is about **deep connection** — how to make a relationship not merely "error-free," but one where the other genuinely feels: "With you, I become better." That is the core claim of *How to Win Friends and Influence People*: the only right way to win others' favor is to **be genuinely interested in them**, not manipulate with tricks.

## 1. Carnegie's Four Interpersonal Laws

- **Six ways to be liked**: sincere interest, smile often, remember names, listen attentively, talk about what interests them, make them feel important.
- **Twelve ways to gain agreement**: avoid arguments, admit you might be wrong, empathize first, get them saying "yes," make the idea feel like theirs.
- **Nine ways to change others without offense**: sincere praise before advice, ask instead of command, save their face.
- **Seven ways to a happy family**: no criticism, sincere appreciation, notice small things, treat with courtesy.

> In a relationship, translated to one line: **do not rush to show yourself; first make the other feel "with her, she becomes better."**

## 2. The Five Love Languages

Gary Chapman's classic framework, also the "decoder" of deep connection:

1. **Words of affirmation**: praised, thanked.
2. **Quality time**: exclusive, undistracted togetherness.
3. **Receiving gifts**: a thoughtful small object.
4. **Acts of service**: "by the way" solving a hassle for you.
5. **Physical touch**: hugs, holding hands.

Know **your own and the other's primary love language**, state it directly. Many use the wrong channel — you push gifts (your language), she wants "you put the phone down and listen" (quality time).

## 3. Three Trainable Moves

- **Gifting formula**: notice a small hassle she mentioned → on a **non-holiday** ordinary day → quietly solve it → say "just picked this up." Far more impactful than holiday splurging. This is the advanced version of Ch.1's "small-dream fulfillment."
- **Argument rule**: when分歧 (divergent), first say "You have a point, I had not seen it from that angle," cool 70% before discussing the real issue. This is Ch.4's "empathize first" landed in couple arguments.
- **Listening experiment**: next conversation, keep **your talking under 30%**. Only listen, ask for details, remember small things. The person people like is often "the one who really listened to me."

## 4. People-Pleasing vs Sincerity: One Dividing Line

Low-level: please others and suppress yourself to be liked. High-level: **sincere giving under the premise of separation of tasks (Ch.1)** — you are stable, so your kindness "overflows," not "is traded." The line is: when you give, are you filling your own insecurity, or sharing your abundance?

## 5. Chapter Summary

- Deep connection = make the other feel "better with you," via genuine interest not manipulation.
- Five love languages: know your own and theirs, do not use the wrong channel.
- Three moves: gifting formula, argument rule, listen ≤30%.

**In one line**: the highest state of being liked is not "you are eloquent," but "you make me feel I am pretty good myself."
