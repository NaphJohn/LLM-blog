- **Impact**: implicit reasoning now has a "teacher present" paradigm — physics constraints are pressed in during the forward pass, vehicle-side deployment cost drops, and 2B tying 8B means a small model can also swallow "physical thinking".

### Operator Deep-Dive: VGGT Alternating Attention (CVPR 2025 Best Paper)

- **Positioning**: intra-frame↔global attention alternates over 24 layers + camera token; one forward 0.51s yields the full 3D attribute set (depth/pose/point cloud) for 32 views, replacing SfM/BA post-processing.
- **Key point**: a first-frame independent learnable token anchors the world coordinate system; 200 frames ≈40GB VRAM; in use by VGGT itself, LaST-VLA's geometry teacher, and DriveVGGT — a flag-bearer of the "end-to-end 3D prior" school.
